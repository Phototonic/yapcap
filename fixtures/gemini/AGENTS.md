# Gemini Fixtures

- `probe.py` experiments with Google OAuth and Code Assist requests.
- `oauth_token_response.json` and `oauth_token_400_response.json` cover successful and failed token responses.
- `load_code_assist_response.json` captures project and entitlement discovery.
- `retrieve_user_quota_response.json` captures quota buckets consumed by the Gemini provider.
